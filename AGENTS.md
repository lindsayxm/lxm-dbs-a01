GALLERY LANDING PAGE (holds site design iterations):
index.html is a gallery of 25 versions of a one page website. The overall, high level content of each page version will be the same, but they will each have an independent, unique design, look, and feel. 

At the top of the index page the title says ACCELERATED PROTOTYPING: ARTISAN TECHNOLOGY. There are 25 thumbnails laid out in a grid below, 5 per row. After each iteration on the design, that page is saved and a thumbnail is generated from it. Clicking on a thumbnail brings the user to that version of the site. Begin with placeholders (just a box outline will do), then with each iteration, replace the placeholder with a thumbnail. 


SITE (what to build in each iteration): 
V01: Create an interesting one-page site for a cutting edge, bespoke technology company. Use your design skills to make this as interesting as possible. It should "wow" the visitor and really make them feel like this company is on the bleeding edge. The goal of the page is to inform the potential client about the company and it's principal engineer, to generate interest in working on custom hardware and software projects. There are two images in the /img subdirectory - which may be used if helpful, but are not required. 

V02: Known for breathtaking black-and-white design evoking the vastness of the American landscape, especially mountains, forests, rivers, and deserts. Combine dramatic light, deep shadows, sharp details, and carefully composed scenes to create a sense of grandeur and beauty. Technical precision. Celebrate the natural world but also convey a powerful sense of scale, serenity, and wilderness. 

V03: the background is an off white canvas. you can see the texture of the canvas fabric. at the top left of the page is an illustration of a small paint bucket. it's rocking precariously on the edge of a thin line (is it a shelf?). there is no text visible. just the stark canvas and the small paint can with the line under it. the slight rocking of the paint can draws the user to click it. when the user clicks the can, it tips onto it's side. paint spills endlessly - filling most of the canvas and revealing the white lettering which stands out now (it didn't get covered by the paint) this reveals the content of the site. There is ongoing dripping and slight motion which continues to illustrate the spill. evoking overflow of ideas, and endless supply of creative juice. 

V04: create an animation of a noisy static filled signal on one side, passing through a filter and on the other side of the filter, a pure sine wave. Give it a glowy, ethereal vibe. Ethereal design captures delicacy and transcendence. It feels weightless and otherworldly — like a design made of mist or light. This style emphasizes openness, airiness, and subtle beauty, often with soft pastels and faint textures.

V05: Brutalist version - a monotone, bold, and brash. Brutalism in design is raw, bold, and purposefully unrefined. Borrowed from architecture, it rejects polish for stark functionality and visual honesty. In digital, it breaks UI norms to stand out. Monospaced fonts, grayscale palettes, harsh edges, solid blocks, default buttons.
